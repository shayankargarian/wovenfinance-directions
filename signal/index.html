Six products. Three jobs.

Term loans

Lump-sum funding with fixed repayments for expansion, equipment, fit-outs or hiring.

Acquisition finance

Fund the purchase of a practice, rent roll or competitor, sized on platform data.

Line of credit

A revolving limit customers draw, repay and redraw as cash needs change.

Payables finance

Pay suppliers and stock now, repay on terms that match the cash cycle.

Revenue-based finance

Up-front capital repaid as a share of sales as they come in.

Invoice finance

Advances on outstanding invoices, repaid when the client settles.
