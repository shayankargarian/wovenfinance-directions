Six steps, one connection

From integration to revenue share, every step shows inside your product.

  1. Connect your platform

    Embed ready-made components or use the API.

    woven.js

  2. Send applicant data

    Business and transaction data, sent again whenever it changes.

    POST /v1/applicants/

  3. Surface offers

    Pre-qualified offers render in your UI and stay current.

    widget-session

  4. Apply and verify

    A short hosted flow in your brand, prefilled from your data.

    POST /v1/applications/

  5. Fund and repay

    Accept in a few taps, funds land, repayments collect.

    GET /status/

  6. Earn revenue share

    A share of revenue on every loan written through you.

    webhook: loan.funded
