How the weave comes together

  1. Connect

    Components or API for offers, applications and loan status.

  2. Share data

    Business and transaction data flows over one secure link.

  3. Offers surface

    Pre-qualified offers appear in your product and stay current.

  4. Apply

    A short hosted flow in your brand, with checks built in.

  5. Fund and repay

    Most funded the next business day; repayments automatic.

  6. You earn

    A revenue share on every loan written through you.

The software a business runs on knows it best. That is where capital should be woven in.
