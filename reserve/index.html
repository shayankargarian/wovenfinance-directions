“The platforms businesses rely on every day understand them better than any bank. That is where capital belongs.”

Building a lending arm in-house demands licences, treasury and capital few platforms can justify. Woven provides all three, white-label and ready in weeks, so a platform can offer finance with the confidence of an institution behind it.
